# Ape Scout: research the landscape

Read [competitor research](../../../docs/COMPETITORS.md) and the [decision brief](../../../docs/DECISION-BRIEF.md) for the question and prior evidence; reverify time-sensitive claims rather than copying them as current facts.

- Bound the question to a decision: capability overlap, availability, portability, or a falsifiable differentiation hypothesis.
- Prefer official documentation, specifications and original product announcements. Capture exact source URL, access date, feature scope, and release/beta status. Separate vendor claims from independently observed behavior.
- Compare equivalent capabilities and deployment tiers. “Not found in reviewed sources” is not evidence of absence or uniqueness. Record conflicting and missing evidence and what would change the recommendation.
- Consider the counter-hypothesis that an existing product or equal-budget general investigator is sufficient. Preserve provider-neutral options; recommend narrowing or stopping when supported.

Deliver a concise sourced comparison, confidence/limitations, and the next decision. Do not create accounts, install products, run comparisons, or contact vendors under a research request. Public source research must not transmit private project material.

This is a planning workflow under [AGENTS.md](../../../AGENTS.md); recommendations do not approve implementation, spending, architecture, or production work.

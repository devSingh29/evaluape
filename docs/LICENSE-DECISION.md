# License decision: pending owner approval

**No license has been selected or applied. Do not add a LICENSE file until the owner approves the license and copyright-holder details.** Intended open source does not itself grant reuse rights. This is a planning comparison, not legal advice.

## Options

| Consideration | MIT | Apache-2.0 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| General approach | Short permissive license allowing broad reuse, modification, distribution, and sale. | Permissive license with more detailed copyright, patent, and redistribution terms. |
| Redistribution | Preserve the copyright and permission notices in copies or substantial portions. | Supply the license, identify modified files, preserve relevant notices, and carry applicable NOTICE attributions when present. |
| Patents | Text has no express patent-license clause. | Express contributor patent grant for covered claims, with a patent-litigation termination provision. |
| Trademarks | No explicit trademark section in the text. | Does not grant general trademark rights. |
| Practical trade-off | Simpler notice handling and shorter text. | More explicit patent treatment with additional compliance details. |

Sources: [OSI's MIT license text](https://opensource.org/license/mit) and [Apache Software Foundation's Apache License 2.0](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0), reviewed 2026-10-17. Both disclaim warranties and limit liability, subject to their terms.

## Conditional recommendation

Prefer **Apache-2.0** if Evaluape is intended to attract organizational contributors and explicit contributor patent terms are an important project goal. Prefer **MIT** if the owner prioritizes simplicity and accepts its less explicit patent treatment. Neither choice guarantees patent clearance or resolves ownership of material contributed by others.

## Approval checklist

- [ ] Confirm the project owner and actual copyright holder; account name alone is insufficient.
- [ ] Choose MIT or Apache-2.0 explicitly.
- [ ] Confirm all included material can be distributed under the chosen terms.
- [ ] Decide how documentation, fixtures, and future contributions will be covered.
- [ ] Review dependency and third-party attribution requirements when dependencies exist.
- [ ] After approval, add the unmodified official license text with appropriate holder/year details where applicable, and assess NOTICE requirements if choosing Apache-2.0.
- [ ] Update the README and contribution policy to reflect the actual decision.
